### About me

I design and ship internal automation systems (HR, finance, payroll, reporting and project management) built on LLM-assisted pipelines, Python services and data infrastructure.

Over four years at **Tabdeal**, one of Iran's largest crypto exchanges, I've moved across backend engineering, the AI team, marketing & PR leadership, and now automation engineering. That path lets me build automation around how teams actually work, not just around what is technically possible.

### What I'm working on

- ⚙️ **HR, Finance & Salary Automation Systems**: replacing manual onboarding, leave, payroll and reconciliation workflows with tracked pipelines
- 📊 **Auto Data-Driven System**: scheduled analytics generated and delivered to stakeholders (Python, SQL, Metabase)
- 🤖 **LLM components in production**: classification, extraction and summarisation with review steps and audit trails for sensitive processes
